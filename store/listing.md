## Full description (≤4000 chars)

Mensura turns your phone into a precise, private pocket measurement toolkit — no account, no internet, no tracking. Every tool works offline and every reading stays on your device.

**FREE TOOLS**

• **Level** — a spirit level with a bullseye and dual-axis readout (left/right + front/back), haptic feedback, and one-tap zero calibration.
• **Compass** — a live magnetic compass with heading in degrees and cardinal direction.
• **Converter** — convert length, weight, volume, temperature, area, speed, and time.
• **Tip Split** — calculate tips and split the bill, with presets and per-person breakdown.
• **Stopwatch** — a lap timer with split times.

**MENSURA PRO** (one-time unlock, no subscription)

• **Ruler** — measure on screen in centimeters or inches, with credit-card calibration for accuracy.
• **Protractor** — measure any angle with a draggable arm.
• **Flashlight** — turn your camera flash into a torch, with a strobe mode.
• **QR Scanner** — scan QR codes and open or share the result instantly.
• **Altimeter** — read air pressure and relative altitude with the built-in barometer.

**PRIVATE BY DESIGN**

Mensura requires no account and sends nothing to any server. Camera, motion, and pressure sensors are used only on-device, and no measurement is ever stored or shared. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Upgrade to Pro once and unlock the full toolkit forever.
