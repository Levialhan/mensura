Mensura — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

No data collected

Mensura is built on a simple principle: your data stays on your device. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not collect.

Data we collect

None. Mensura does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are included.
  • Nothing you do in the app is sent to any server.

How the app uses your device

Mensura uses certain device capabilities to provide its tools. All of this happens locally and is never recorded or transmitted:

  • Camera — used only for the Flashlight (torch) and QR Scanner tools. The camera feed is processed on-device and is never saved or uploaded.
  • Motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer) — used by the Level and Compass tools to compute tilt and heading in real time. No readings are stored.
  • Barometer — used by the Altimeter tool to estimate air pressure and relative altitude. No readings are stored.

Permissions

The app may request camera access when you use the Flashlight or QR Scanner. You can decline this and continue using the other tools.

Children's privacy

Mensura does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new "last updated" date.
